Little Today: Mood Diary

Privacy Policy

Your daily entries belong to you. Little Today is designed to keep your mood stickers, notes, and photos on your device, with optional sync through your private iCloud account.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Information stored by the app

Little Today does not require you to create an account. Mood entries, stickers, notes, monthly notes, attached photos, and app preferences are stored on your device. The app does not send this content to servers operated by the developer.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, your entries, monthly notes, and attached photos may sync through Apple CloudKit to the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. This feature is optional and can be turned off in the app’s settings. Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not operate the iCloud service or receive a copy of your synced content.

Apple Privacy Policy

Photos and backup files

When you choose a photo, the app stores it with that day’s entry on your device. If iCloud Sync is enabled, the photo may sync with the entry as described above.

When you export a backup, Little Today creates a ZIP file on your device containing your entries and any attached photos. You choose where to save or share that file. A copy is not sent to the developer.

Purchases and notifications

Optional in-app purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. The app receives purchase and entitlement information needed to provide the purchased features; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

Daily reminders are scheduled as local notifications by iOS on your device. Little Today does not send reminder content to a notification server.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

Little Today does not include advertising or analytics tools, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. The developer does not receive your entries or photos. iCloud sync and App Store purchases are provided by Apple as described in this policy.

Retention and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you remove the app and its local data. If iCloud Sync is enabled, removing the app may not remove data already synced to iCloud. You can manage iCloud storage and data through your Apple Account settings. You can turn off iCloud Sync in Little Today, decline to attach photos, and disable reminders in the app or iOS Settings.

Contact

If you email support, the developer receives the email address and information you include so they can respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact yui480145@gmail.com.

隐私政策

你的每日记录由你掌控。「今日份」会将心情贴纸、文字和照片保存在设备本地；你也可以选择通过自己的 iCloud 账号同步。

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

App 保存的信息

使用「今日份」不需要创建账号。每日记录、贴纸、文字、月便签、添加的照片和 App 设置保存在你的设备上。App 不会将这些内容发送到开发者运营的服务器。

可选的 iCloud 同步

开启 iCloud 同步后，记录、月便签和照片可能通过 Apple CloudKit 同步到与你的 Apple 账号关联的私有 iCloud 数据库。此功能由你选择，可在 App 设置中关闭。Apple 按其条款和隐私政策处理 iCloud 数据；开发者不运营 iCloud 服务，也不会收到同步内容的副本。

Apple 隐私政策

照片与备份文件

选择照片后，App 会将照片与当天的记录一起保存在设备上。开启 iCloud 同步后，照片可能会随记录一起同步。

导出备份时，「今日份」会在设备上创建 ZIP 文件，包含每日记录和已添加的照片。你可以自行选择保存或分享的位置；开发者不会收到备份副本。

购买与通知

可选的 App 内购买由 Apple App Store 通过 StoreKit 处理。App 会收到提供已购功能所需的购买和权益信息；开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息。

每日提醒由 iOS 在你的设备上安排为本地通知。「今日份」不会将提醒内容发送到通知服务器。

分析、广告与分享

「今日份」不包含广告或分析工具，不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你，也不会出售个人信息。开发者不会收到你的每日记录或照片。iCloud 同步和 App Store 购买由 Apple 按本政策所述提供。

保存期限与选择

本地 App 数据会保留在设备上，直到你移除 App 及其本地数据。若已开启 iCloud 同步，移除 App 不一定会删除已同步到 iCloud 的数据。你可以在 Apple 账号设置中管理 iCloud 储存空间和数据。你可以关闭 iCloud 同步、不添加照片，也可以在 App 或 iOS 设置中关闭提醒。

联系我们
