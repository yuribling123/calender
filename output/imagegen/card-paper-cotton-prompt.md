# Card paper cotton texture

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original bitmap is copied intact into `MoodCalendar/Resources/Assets.xcassets/cardPaperCottonWeave.imageset/cotton-weave.png`.

The neutral texture is shared by the cream cotton ground, gray cotton ground, and dusty-rose heart masks. Repeat spacing, motif shapes, colors, and appearance adaptation are controlled in SwiftUI.

## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square textile texture for a small SwiftUI mood journal background and fabric heart applique masks. Generate a flat orthographic scan of real very fine plain-weave cotton fabric, neutral light silver ivory (almost white, zero blue or yellow cast), tiny clearly authentic interlaced cotton threads and a few extremely subtle natural fibers. Uniform diffuse lighting, even luminance over the entire square, no directional shading, no vignette, no folds or wrinkles, no perspective, no objects, no motifs, no hearts, no text, no seams, no borders. Edge-to-edge seamless tile, every edge must tile naturally. Textile weave microscopic and delicate, low contrast, soft matte surface, suitable to tint dusty rose for fabric hearts or retain as cream/gray cotton under readable UI text. 1024x1024 texture swatch, not a fabric product photograph.
